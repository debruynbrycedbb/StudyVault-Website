> **The central finding:** the brand has built a large, mostly Polish Instagram audience but not the digital infrastructure for its newest and biggest bet, the Gràcia boutique. The site is in Polish (EN/ES are machine translations), prices stay in złoty, the Barcelona address and hours appear nowhere on the site, and the boutique account has 343 followers.

- **Market:** second-hand is mainstream and growing in both countries. Poland's second-hand clothing market grew ≈34% a year over six years, mostly through apps like Vinted. Catalonia received a record 20.1m foreign tourists in 2025.
- **Audience:** likely urban women aged 25–34 who already shop second-hand and want unique pieces. Price is the main reason Europeans who want to shop sustainably don't.
- **Competition:** direct rivals are small upcycling designers (Couple Dansant in Poland; Tania Marcial, Owl and Sylvia Calvo around Barcelona). The bigger threat is indirect: cheap resale apps and ultra-fast fashion.
- **Instagram:** 54 Reels in 4.5 months (≈2.8/week), median 27.4k views. Five Reels produced 65% of all 8.8m views. Celebrity dressing, festivals, humour and creator collabs travel furthest; paid partnerships get the weakest response per view; Barcelona Reels get the fewest views of all.
- **Regulation:** the revised EU Waste Framework Directive makes textile producers pay for collection and recycling (schemes due by April 2028) — a B2B story to sell.

**Recommended focus:** 1) make the brand findable and buyable in Barcelona (human-edited EN/ES/CA site, EUR prices, boutique page, Google Business Profile) · 2) turn the boutique into an experience (in-store upcycling workshops) · 3) bring proven Polish formats to Barcelona with EN/ES captions and local co-authors.

## Brand snapshot and milestones

Started in 2017 when the founder (a former competitive swimmer) remade his grandmother's clothes into a fringed festival jacket. Every piece is made from existing materials. Homepage impact claim: 55 million litres of water and 147 tonnes of CO₂ saved since 2017.

## Target audience

**Primary:** urban adults ≈22–40, majority women, in Polish cities and Barcelona; creative, value individuality; buy for occasions; discover on Reels. Barriers: price (41%), not knowing where to buy (24%), doubts about sustainability claims (19%).

**Secondary:** Barcelona visitors (≈€221/day, culture-seeking) · companies and event agencies (B2B workshops for 50–150 people) · brands seeking a circular story (e.g. billboard bags for Bracia Sadownicy).

## Competitive landscape

**Direct (upcycling designers):** Couple Dansant (PL) · Monika Surowiec (PL) · Tania Marcial (BCN) · Owl (BCN, Poblenou) · Sylvia Calvo (Castelldefels) · Nathalia García (BCN).

**Benchmarks from the award cohort:** Canto Primo (IT) — "structured upcycling", 40 scalable carry-over designs · MARTAN (NL) — one clear material story (discarded hotel linen).

**Indirect:** resale platforms (Vinted, OLX, Allegro Lokalnie) · ultra-fast fashion · sustainable and vintage boutiques in Barcelona · big retailers entering circularity (H&M's Sellpy, 4F, LPP with Wtórpol).

> **Insight:** the brand's gap isn't credibility but repeatability and accessibility — four products online, złoty-only checkout, and no core range a visitor can simply buy.

## Digital presence audit

**Website:** content in Polish; EN/ES via machine translation with no indexable pages (Spanish "Sklep" became "Almacenar"); Barcelona boutique info missing; 4 products online; workshops "coming soon"; impact counter shows "+0 KG" to crawlers; stale embedded Instagram feed.

**Instagram (@jackobbuczynski):** 96.9k followers · 2.8 Reels/week · median 27.4k views (28% of followers) · 5 Reels = 65% of views.

- Product posts are the steady base (median 27.9k) and draw buying questions answered only by DM.
- Paid partnerships buy views, not interest (0.7% engagement vs 2.4% account median).
- Barcelona content is the weakest (median 7.9k) but has the highest engagement per view (3.6%): a distribution problem.

**Boutique account (@jackobbuczynskigarage):** 343 followers, 13 posts; own Reels got 431, 649 and 759 views.

## Opportunities (priority 5 = act first)

1. **Barcelona-ready website** (human-edited EN/ES/CA, EUR, boutique page, Google Business Profile, local SEO) — 5
2. **Boutique as experience:** bookable upcycling workshops for tourists, locals and teams — 5
3. **Take proven reach formats to Barcelona** with Spanish creators and EN/ES captions — 4
4. **Turn Instagram demand into sales:** prices in captions, product tags, Highlights — 4
5. **B2B circular services** for brands (workshops, branded materials, EPR storytelling) — 4
6. **A small carry-over core range** sold online and in store — 3

**Recommended primary opportunity:** Barcelona as a findable, bookable destination (1 + 2).

## Risks

Price gap vs resale and ultra-fast fashion (high/high) · founder dependency (high/medium) · running two locations (medium/high) · tourism plateau and seasonality · sustainability claims without a public method · supply concentration on Wtórpol · local competition · EPR obligations.
