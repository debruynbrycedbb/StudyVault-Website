# Deliverable 1 — What it must cover

**Master's Thesis · RD1** · Checklist from *Thesis deliverable 1.docx*

## Company description

- General company information
- Activity description
- Sector in which it operates
- Context for understanding the company

## General company information

- Organisation structure, culture, know-how
- Resources: human, tech, financial
- Revenue
- Customers: profile, segmentation, ABC, satisfaction and loyalty level
- Market share by products and segment, evolution
- Competitive advantage

## Description of company activity

- Product portfolio, breadth, depth, brand portfolio, image and positioning, brand strategy
- Distribution: channels used, coverage, logistics and capabilities, sales policy
- Communication: messages, target audience, media and supports used

## Sector description

- Market: market definition, geographical delimitation
- Determinants: PEST factors, size, growth, trends, seasonality etc.
- Competitors' presence
- Competitors' digital activity

## Context

- 3 buyer personas
- Distinguish if the persona is a **brand lover**, **loyal** or **occasional**
- Consumer: profile, segmentation, purchasing decision process, roles in the buying process, buying habits, consumption or usage habits, purchase motivation
- Competition: direct and indirect, substitute products, competitive structure, main competitors

## The opportunity

Clearly define the opportunity we are pursuing with the plan:

- What are we creating with the marketing plan?
- What do we want to achieve?
- Provide **quantified** answers to these questions.
