## 0 · Summary

| Instagram followers on @jackobbuczynski, mostly a Polish audience | followers on the boutique account @jackobbuczynskigarage | median views of September Barcelona Reels vs 27.4k account median | boutique hours a week vs 40–60 h at Gràcia vintage shops |

A Polish upcycling brand founded in 2017 that turns second-hand textiles into one-of-a-kind fashion. Run by its founder from a Warsaw atelier, it sources from Poland's largest textile sorter Wtórpol, has shown at fashion weeks in New York, Berlin and Budapest, and in 2026 opened its first boutique in Gràcia, supported by a 141,048 zł Polish government grant. It is strong in Poland but barely visible in Barcelona: Barcelona content has lost reach, the boutique account is small, no Google Maps listing was found, and the website doesn't show the shop's address, hours or euro prices.

> **The opportunity:** turn an internationally recognised designer brand into a known Barcelona destination by making the boutique findable, repositioning it from "vintage/thrift shop" to upcycling atelier and experience, and building a local Instagram audience.

## 3 · Company activity

- **Portfolio:** 4 product lines (signature jackets, collections, accessories, bespoke) plus services (online course; studio, festival and B2B workshops). Only 4 products listed online.
- **Brand portfolio:** one founder-centric master brand with sub-labels — ReMade Barcelona (collection), "Garage" (boutique account), #byJackob (bespoke).
- **Positioning gap:** the brand says "Reborn through upcycling, design and stories", but the boutique's October posts use #vintagebarcelona and #thriftbarcelona, which places it among low-price vintage shops.
- **Distribution:** WooCommerce shop (złoty, up to 14 working days delivery) · Warsaw atelier · Barcelona boutique Wed–Sun 15:00–20:00 · Instagram DMs · festivals and pop-ups · B2B by email.
- **Communication:** celebrity/press (median ≈300k views) and festival Reels (≈270k) travel furthest; account median 27.4k. Barcelona content fell from ≈25–28k views (opening, English captions) to 7.9k (September, Polish captions) to under 800 on the boutique account. Paid partnership posts: median 25k views but 0.7% engagement per view.

## 5 · Context

**Personas** (hypotheses): **Kasia, 28** — brand lover, Polish creative in Barcelona (role: ambassador) · **Giulia, 31** — loyal, Italian designer in Gràcia (core recurring revenue) · **Emma, 29** — occasional, US visitor on a 4-day trip (peak-season volume, reviews). A B2B events lead is treated as a segment.

**Decision process:** need → search (Instagram, Google Maps, walking past) → evaluation (uniqueness vs price, story, reviews) → purchase (in store or booking, EUR, card) → post-purchase (tags, stories, reviews).

**Competition:** direct — CRYPTA, Tania Marcial, Vendimia (medium threat) · indirect — Gràcia vintage shops, old-town cluster, sustainable boutiques (high) · substitutes — Vinted, Wallapop, ultra-fast fashion, free public workshops (medium–high on price). Structure: fragmented, no dominant designer-upcycling player in Barcelona.

## 6 · The opportunity

**What the plan creates:** findability (Google Business Profile, EN/ES/CA Barcelona page with hours, map, euro prices, workshop booking) · repositioning to "upcycling atelier and experience" · a local Instagram content system in EN/ES · a calendar around Primavera Sound, Festes de Gràcia and 080 Barcelona Fashion · a measured funnel from discovery to visit to purchase or booking.
