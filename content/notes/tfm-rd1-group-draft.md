## Executive summary

Jackob Buczynski is a Polish upcycling fashion brand founded in 2017. The brand transforms second-hand textiles into unique, handmade clothing and accessories. It operates from a Warsaw atelier and opened its first international boutique in Barcelona's Gràcia district in 2026.

Its business combines sustainable and circular fashion, handmade one-of-a-kind designs, custom-made clothing, creative workshops and brand collaborations, and digital communication and social media.

> **The main challenge:** despite an established international reputation and a strong Polish Instagram audience, the brand remains relatively unknown among Barcelona residents and visitors.
>
> **The main opportunity:** establish the Barcelona boutique as a recognisable destination for designer upcycling, creative experiences and sustainable fashion.

## Barcelona market analysis

**Market definition:** designer upcycled fashion and creative fashion experiences within Barcelona, particularly Gràcia.

- **Primary market:** Gràcia and neighbouring districts
- **Secondary market:** Barcelona residents and international visitors
- **Digital market:** Spanish and international customers reached through social media and the online store

## The main communication problem

The brand has a strong digital identity in Poland, but its communication isn't adapted to the Barcelona audience. Barcelona content has declined in reach: approximate median Reel views fell from **25–28K** in the opening phase to **7.9K** in September 2026. The boutique needs a more consistent, locally relevant content strategy.

### Four strategic priorities

1. **Visibility:** make the boutique easier to find through Google Business Profile, Maps and local SEO.
2. **Positioning:** move away from the vintage/thrift-shop perception towards designer upcycling and a creative atelier.
3. **Local community:** build a Barcelona-focused Instagram audience through English and Spanish content.
4. **Experience:** use workshops, events and creator collaborations to encourage physical visits.

## Marketing opportunity and objectives

**Strategic vision:** transform the boutique into a recognised destination for designer upcycling, sustainable fashion and creative experiences in Barcelona.

The plan should: increase local brand awareness · generate more visits to the Gràcia boutique · increase boutique sales · build a loyal Barcelona-based audience · increase workshop bookings · establish the brand as a reference for designer upcycling in Barcelona.

## Conclusion

The brand already has an established designer identity, international recognition, distinctive products and an existing digital audience. Its Barcelona boutique faces challenges in local awareness, digital visibility, accessibility and communication, so the strategy should convert digital attention into real-life boutique visits, purchases and workshop bookings.

> **The central strategic idea:** from a recognised Polish upcycling brand to a must-visit creative fashion destination in Barcelona.
